This index documents the existing public evidence and the subsequent verification work. It is not a journal acceptance notice or a new Zenodo deposit.

## Public archives

- [Zenodo EP11, version DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24480002](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24480002): published research archive dated 23 November 2026. The project representative confirmed that the DOI opened without signing in on 27 November. - [EP20/EP21 public evidence](R21R8_EP21_PUBLIC/README.md): Bristol reproduction code and aggregate results, all-configuration support audit, exact OOF records, and a separately labelled exploratory diagnostic.
