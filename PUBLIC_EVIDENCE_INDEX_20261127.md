# H3Air reproducibility evidence index — 27 November 2026

This index documents the existing public evidence and the subsequent verification work. It is not a journal acceptance notice or a new Zenodo deposit.

## Public archives

- [Zenodo EP11, version DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24480002](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24480002): published research archive dated 23 November 2026. The project representative confirmed that the DOI opened without signing in on 27 November. The landing-page inventory lists 40 reconstructed bootstrap-state task ZIPs, code, manifests and component-specific rights. The complete Zenodo payload was not independently downloaded in this update.
- [EP11 GitHub release](../../releases/tag/h3air-r21r8-ep11-research-v1): 40 reconstructed-state task assets; all 40 have recorded SHA-256 release digests. These are newly fitted states, not recovered original historical internal-state bytes.
- [EP18 AF4 and AF5](../../releases/tag/h3air-r21r8-ep18-evidence-v1): derivative and critical-budget evidence, contextual budget sensitivity, selection and fold contrasts.
- [EP20/EP21 public evidence](R21R8_EP21_PUBLIC/README.md): Bristol reproduction code and aggregate results, all-configuration support audit, exact OOF records, and a separately labelled exploratory diagnostic.
- [NASA relearning and witness transport](PUBLIC_NASA_RELEARNING_TRANSPORT_AUDIT/README.md): 7,998/8,000 successful relearning attempts, 11.612 dB matched local-function disagreement, and 69/107 original strict reversal witnesses retaining the held-out prediction budget.

## Additional verification and access boundaries

The subsequent author-review evidence collection contains a historical R5 stored-output selection (80 branch NPZs, 40 parent NPZs, 285 original payloads verified), a concrete factorial recovery/replay audit (20 historical outer models and 10 newly reconstructed outer models; six RMSE scenarios matching previously reported precision; 360 slope-budget decisions), and Bristol finite-design collision and universal-feasibility audit materials. The complete private collection is not published by this index. Distribution of mixed-source concrete files and detailed Bristol-derived records requires component-level rights review; raw Bristol data should be acquired from the [provider](https://doi.org/10.5523/bris.30c8q39wi1mxf205flnun3l5rl) under its own terms.

Historical original per-draw antecedent states and first-download UCI bytes remain unavailable. The Zenodo EP11 archive does not include all subsequent evidence. Original project materials and upstream datasets retain their respective rights; see [EP11 licence scope](R21R8_EP11_PUBLIC/LICENSE_SCOPE.md) and [EP21 licence scope](R21R8_EP21_PUBLIC/LICENSE_SCOPE.md).

No private reviewer correspondence, current manuscript, restricted data, or unapproved mixed-source archive is distributed by this update. No scientific results are changed.
