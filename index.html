What this tool does—and does not certify

The browser uses a conservative float64 projection/dual solver. On the 510 archived primary NASA coefficients, it gave 487 conclusive decisions (397 protected, 90 reversible), all matching the paper's classifications, and abstained on 23. The separate Python implementation resolves the full 510-case reference. No physical, continuous-domain, industrial safety or causal guarantee is implied.
