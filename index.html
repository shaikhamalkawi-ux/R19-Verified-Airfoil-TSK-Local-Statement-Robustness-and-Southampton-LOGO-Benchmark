Scope and verification

Web v0.1.1 generated and independently verified all 510 archived primary NASA decisions: 403 protected and 107 reversible, with no unresolved or incorrect conclusive decisions. A separate check on 120 official-UCI Concrete decisions returned 118 protected and 2 reversible, all independently checked. These checks use frozen models and do not replace the paper's historical witnesses. Unresolved remains a valid outcome for other inputs.

The audit applies to the supplied fixed basis and finite training design. It does not certify physical causality, industrial safety, a continuous input domain, or antecedent relearning. An unresolved result is not evidence of protection.

For unresolved cases, use the full Python solver. Original NASA/UCI source: Brooks, Pope and Marcolini, Airfoil Self-Noise. Download offline HTML · NASA verification record · Concrete verification record · Earlier research archive (EP11).
